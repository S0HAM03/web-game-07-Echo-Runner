How to Play
Master the Echo Runner

🎮 CONTROLS

• W / Up Arrow — Move outward (outer lane)

• S / Down Arrow — Move inward (inner lane)

• Mobile — Tap In / Out buttons

🏆 GOAL & MECHANICS

• Your runner sprints left around the circular track

• Every 5 seconds an Echo Ghost spawns and replays your exact path

• Rocks = instant death — dodge them by switching lanes!

• Collect golden coins to unlock colors & themes in the Shop

• Speed bumps up every 4 laps — brace yourself!
